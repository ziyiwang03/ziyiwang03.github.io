I am interested in how discrete models preserve the structure, stability, and physical meaning of continuous systems.

A model equation connecting transport, diffusion, and forcing is

\[
  \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot F(u)
  = \nabla \cdot \bigl(D(u)\nabla u\bigr) + f.
\]
